Our Story

It began at the University of Lagos, with two engineers in the making. He studied Civil Engineering and I studied Electrical Engineering. One of us knew how to hold a building up, and the other knew how to light it. We were both heading into our third year, with no idea that the best project of our lives was about to begin.

A few weeks before our real beginning, Ope picked me up at the school gate and dropped me at the faculty. Not one word passed between us in that car. But, as I would learn later, he had already noticed me around the faculty, and he had already decided he liked me.

Then came a day in the second semester of third year, exactly one month and one day after my birthday. It is a detail Ope remembers without a second of hesitation. I was walking to the nail shop with my friends when he drove past with a friend, on his way home. His friend begged him to stay in school a little longer, and Ope said the only reason he would stay was if he saw me. And there I was.

He stopped the car. He stayed with my friends and me until the very last nail was done, and then we all went for pizza. It was the first slice of a story we are still writing.

Since that day, life has taken us a long way. We finished our first degrees, then both served our country in Lagos for NYSC, and then we set off for our master's degrees in the UK.

Like every couple, we have had our ups and downs. But one thing has always been certain: we love each other, and we are willing to make it work in every kind of weather.

Now we are getting married, and we want to grow old and grey together, till death do us part. We would love for you to be there with us.
